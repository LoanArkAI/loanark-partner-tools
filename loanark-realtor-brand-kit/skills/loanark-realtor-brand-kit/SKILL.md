# Loan Ark Realtor Brand Kit

One short interview, one file. The file is `realtor-brand-profile.md`, and the other Loan Ark skills (listing marketing, buyer explainers, client updates, market pulse, social content) read it so every draft sounds like the agent and carries the right license lines and the right Loan Ark loan officer. Ten minutes once, then never re-explain yourself.

Read `references/brand-and-compliance.md` before you start. It has the co-brand block and the rules the profile has to satisfy.

## Step 1 — Ask for what is missing, in one message

Ask only for what the agent has not already given you, grouped into one message so it feels like a form, not an interrogation. Skip anything they already mentioned. The essentials:

1. **Name as it appears on their license, and title** (Realtor member? broker or salesperson? team name if any).
2. **Brokerage name as licensed, and the agent's license number** (DRE in California, TREC in Texas, CO License in Colorado). Office city.
3. **Contact they want on materials**: phone, email, website, main social handle.
4. **Their Loan Ark loan officer** (name; the LO's NMLS number if they know it, otherwise leave a placeholder the LO fills in).
5. **Market**: the one city or area they want to be known for online, plus the surrounding areas they actually work. Ask for one primary; explain that one market online beats three.
6. **Who they help most** (first-time buyers, move-up sellers, relocations, VA, luxury, investors) as *needs*, not demographics.
7. **Voice**: three words for how they want to sound, two phrases they say a lot, anything they never want said. If they have past posts or emails, ask them to paste two.
8. **Proof they can back up**: years licensed, homes sold or clients helped (rounded, "150+"), any real award with the year.
9. **Preferred call to action** ("text me", "book a call at…", "reply with your question").

If the agent has an existing profile from the Loan Ark realtor portal's Realtor Brand Brain resource, accept it as the starting point and only ask about the fields above that it lacks (preferred LO, primary market, proof).

## Step 2 — Write the profile

Produce `realtor-brand-profile.md` using the template in `references/profile-template.md`. Rules:

- Mark each fact **confirmed** (the agent said it) or **to confirm** (you inferred it or it is missing). Do not invent credentials, numbers, awards, or expertise.
- Round proof to a floor ("10+ years", "150+ clients") so it stays true.
- Put the co-brand block from `references/brand-and-compliance.md` at the bottom, filled in.
- Keep "Never say" as a real list the other skills can obey (words, claims, competitors, rates).
- Voice section includes 3 to 5 sample sentences in their voice, written by you from their answers, so other skills have something to imitate.

## Step 3 — Hand it over

Give the agent the file, then two sentences: what to confirm (usually the license line and the LO's NMLS) and how to use it ("attach this file, or keep it in the folder you connect, when you use the other Loan Ark skills"). Offer to save it where they keep their work if a folder is connected.

## Updating

When the agent gives changes, apply them to the profile they attached, show what changed, and return the complete file. Never restructure the file when updating.

## What this skill refuses

Inventing credentials, license numbers, awards, sales numbers, or reviews. Writing demographic or protected-class descriptions of "ideal clients" (reframe as needs). Adding any Loan Ark rate or payment language. Anything in the "never" lists of `references/brand-and-compliance.md`.
